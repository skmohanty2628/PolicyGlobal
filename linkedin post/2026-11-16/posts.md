## Post 1 (post1.png): US mortgage rates

Mortgage rates have fallen four weeks in a row. Here is what that is worth in actual dollars.

• At 7.11% today: about $2,691 a month

• At February's 5.98% low: about $2,393

So the recent drop saves about $79 a month. Welcome, but the payment is still about $298 higher than at the start of the year.

## Post 2 (post2.png): India FD real returns

Is your fixed deposit actually growing your money?

India's retail inflation hit 6.09% in October. We ran the numbers on SBI's 6.25% one-year FD:

• Before tax: real return of about 0.15%
• 20% tax bracket: about minus 1.0%
• 30% tax bracket: about minus 1.6%

On Rs 1 lakh, a 30% taxpayer earns about Rs 4,375 after tax while prices rise by about Rs 6,090. FDs are still safe and simple. They just aren't beating inflation right now for most taxpayers.

## Post 4 (post4.png): Texas roof-age rule

Myth: an old roof means your Texas home insurer can drop you.
Fact: insurers can't non-renew on roof age alone. Condition is what counts.

But here is the catch most homeowners miss:
• Many policies pay less for older roofs, or only actual cash value
• Wind and hail deductibles are usually a percentage of your coverage
• On a $400,000 home, a 3% deductible means you pay the first $12,000

Do you know your hail deductible in dollars, not percent?
