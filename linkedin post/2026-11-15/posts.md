## Post 1 (post1.png): US car insurance CPI

Car insurance prices in the US just turned up again.

From May to September, the government's car insurance price index fell about 4.6%. In October it rose 0.7%.

• Repair prices never stopped rising: up about 5% to 6% a year
• Comparison data show the national average roughly flat, with 27 states up in the first half
• October alone, compounded over a year, would be close to 9% (not a forecast, just the pace)

If your policy renews in the next few months, this may be the last good window to shop around. When did you last compare quotes?

## Post 2 (post2.png): India life insurers H1 FY27

India's four biggest listed life insurers made about 25% more from new business in the first half of FY27. But they did it in very different ways.

✅ LIC: value of new business up about 34%, sales up under 6%. The margin did the work.
✅ SBI Life and HDFC Life: growth came almost entirely from selling more
✅ ICICI Life: in between, with margin up to 26%

A higher margin is good news for shareholders. It says nothing about whether a policy is good value for the buyer.

Which matters more to you when choosing a life insurer: claim record, price or brand?

## Post 3 (post3.png): Lemonade, Root, Hippo

Three insurtechs. Three completely different answers to the same question: how do you make a digital insurer profitable?

• Root: profitable, but premium down 8% as it walks away from low-return growth
• Hippo: premium up 60%, but it keeps only about 30% of the risk
• Lemonade: premium up 34%, keeping more of its own risk, still losing money

Our take: the market no longer rewards growth alone. Which model do you think wins over the next five years?
