## Post 1 (image: post1.png)

Your savings balance can go up while what it buys goes down.

We wrote a plain guide to real return: what your interest is worth after inflation and tax.

✔ Real return = (1 + interest) / (1 + inflation) - 1
✔ $10,000 at 4% for 10 years grows to $14,908, but is worth about $11,093 in today's money at 3% inflation
✔ Left at 0.5%, the same $10,000 buys only about $7,822 worth after 10 years

When did you last check what your savings account actually pays?
