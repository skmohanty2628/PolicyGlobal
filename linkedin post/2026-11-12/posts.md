## Post 6: IRDAI motor

Image: post6.png

```
Starting December 15, an Indian motor policy will show you what each part of it costs.

IRDAI has ordered a four-layer structure for private car and two-wheeler policies, with standard wording and a separate premium for each layer.

The four layers:
✔ Third-party liability, still compulsory
✔ Legal liability for occupants or pillion riders, optional
✔ Personal accident cover for the owner
✔ Own damage, optional

Existing third-party customers can add occupant cover mid-term without cancelling. Will clearer pricing change what people choose to buy?
```
